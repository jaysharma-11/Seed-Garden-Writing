Delivery & Fulfillment Policy

Seed Garden Writings provides personalized digital writing. This page explains how an order moves from request to delivery.

Effective September 5, 2026

1. Order confirmation

After a request is successfully submitted, an order ID is created. A request is ready for fulfillment only after required details and any applicable payment have been received. Keep your order ID for questions about the request.

2. Review and clarification

We review the story type, page length, request details, and contact information before writing begins. If essential details are unclear or missing, we may contact you by email. Delivery timing may begin after the needed clarification is received.

3. Estimated timing

Because each piece is personalized, the delivery estimate depends on length, complexity, current request volume, and whether clarification is needed. An expected delivery window should be confirmed after review rather than assumed from the date of submission.

4. Digital delivery

Completed writing is delivered digitally to the email address provided with the order. No physical book or printed product is shipped unless a separate written arrangement explicitly says otherwise. Please check spam or promotions folders if an expected email is not visible.

5. Incorrect contact information

You are responsible for providing a complete and accurate email address. If delivery fails because the submitted address is incorrect, provide the order ID and corrected address so redelivery can be considered.

6. Revisions and fulfillment

An order is marked fulfilled after the completed piece has been sent. Requests for corrections should identify the order and the specific issue. Typographical mistakes or clear omissions from the agreed brief may be corrected; a new direction or major rewrite may require a separate request.

7. Delays

If fulfillment is delayed by an unexpected event, high demand, technical issues, or a need for additional information, we will aim to communicate a revised estimate using the email supplied with the order.

8. Demo notice

The current website checkout is a browser-based demonstration. It records orders only in the current browser and does not itself charge a payment method or transmit an order to a production fulfillment system.
