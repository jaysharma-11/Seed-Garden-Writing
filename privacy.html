Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Seed Garden Writings collects, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Effective September 5, 2026

1. Information you provide

When you submit a writing request, you may provide your name, email address, story type, page preference, seed package, and the personal details included in your request. Please share only information that is necessary for the writing.

2. How information is used

  • To review, create, manage, and deliver your requested writing.
  • To communicate about questions, revisions, payment, or delivery.
  • To maintain the security, reliability, and operation of the service.
  • To meet legal obligations and address misuse when necessary.

3. Current browser storage

The current demo stores order records in your browser’s local storage so they can appear in the admin portal on that same device and browser. Clearing site data can remove those records. The demo does not itself provide secure server-side order storage or live payment processing.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your personal information. If production services such as hosting, email delivery, analytics, or payment processing are added, information may be shared with those providers only as needed to perform their services and subject to their privacy terms.

5. Retention and security

Information should be kept only for as long as needed to complete requests, maintain records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no browser storage or online transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information where applicable. You can also clear locally stored demo orders through your browser settings. Requests may require identity verification before action is taken.

7. Children’s privacy

This service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A parent or guardian should place any request involving a minor and avoid unnecessary sensitive details.

8. Policy updates

This policy may change as the service and its tools develop. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date on this page.
